# Open-source boundary · 开源边界

What is open (the judgment layer + community styles) vs what stays private (one person's taste, keys, client content).
哪些开源（判断层 + 社区风格），哪些私有（某个人的品味、密钥、客户内容）。

## ✅ In the open repo · 进开源仓
- The **judgment layer**: the per-page density knob, the four axes (density / skeleton / device / material), the illustration levels (L0–L4), the cross-page consistency gate, the design floor, the four hard image rules, the stage-constraints layer, the three-tier selection model (medium → hand → world), and the school-system as an *organizing method*. 判断层与组织法。
- The **delivery self-check**. 交付自检。
- **Community style packs** under `styles/` (CC-BY-4.0) — anyone can add their own. 社区风格包，人人可加。
- Sample decks made **from scratch** for demo. 从零做的演示样张。

## 🔒 Not open · 不进
- **One particular person's taste profile** — the private single source of truth of *someone's* aesthetic. The open repo leaves an empty taste interface for you to fill. 某个人的品味档案（留空接口给你自己填）。
- **Any keys / relays / model credentials.** 任何密钥/中转/模型凭据。
- **Any client content or materials.** 任何客户内容/素材。
- **Brand-locked style bindings** tied to a specific product's identity. 绑定某产品品牌身份的专属风格。
