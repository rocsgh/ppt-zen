# Open-source boundary

What is open (the judgment layer + community styles) vs what stays private (one person's taste, keys, client content).

## ✅ In the open repo
- The **judgment layer**: the per-page density knob, the four axes (density / skeleton / device / material), the illustration levels (L0–L4), the cross-page consistency gate, the design floor, the four hard image rules, the stage-constraints layer, the three-tier selection model (medium → hand → world), and the school-system as an *organizing method*.
- The **delivery self-check**.
- **Community style packs** under `styles/` (CC-BY-4.0) — anyone can add their own.
- Sample decks made **from scratch** for demo.

## 🔒 Not open
- **One particular person's taste profile** — the private single source of truth of *someone's* aesthetic. The open repo leaves an empty taste interface for you to fill.
- **Any keys / relays / model credentials.**
- **Any client content or materials.**
- **Brand-locked style bindings** tied to a specific product's identity.
