# Da Vinci Copperplate · 达芬奇铜版

**A page from a genius's secret notebook.** Sepia, intricate, technical — the *device* is complexity made legible: a dense Renaissance machine at the center, light margin studies around it. The hard rule this style tests: decorative marks stay glyph-free (numbers and dimension lines only — no invented Latin).

**天才秘密笔记里的一页。** 棕褐、精密、技术感——器物是"把复杂讲清楚"：中心一台稠密的文艺复兴机器，四周是轻的边角习作。它考验的硬规则：装饰记号必须无字（只有数字和尺寸线，不许自造拉丁字）。
